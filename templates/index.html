    KhetRakshak AI — Product Philosophy & Architecture

    Organized by Startup Cell × AI Club, Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Bhagalpur

    Official Product Description:

    KhetRakshak AI is a Bihar-first agricultural risk intelligence platform that combines Computer Vision, Machine Learning, GIS, weather intelligence and Generative AI to help farmers detect crop-health problems, understand flood and drought risks, and receive personalized, actionable recommendations in an accessible, voice-friendly interface.

    Instead of treating crop disease, weather and climate risks as separate problems, KhetRakshak connects them to understand what environmental conditions mean for a farmer’s specific crop and location.

    The goal is to move agricultural support from reactive diagnosis toward proactive risk awareness and decision support.
